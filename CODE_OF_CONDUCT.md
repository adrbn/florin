# Code of conduct

Florin is a small project run by one person, and it should be a pleasant place to report
a bug, ask a question or send a change — whoever you are and however much you know.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most problems are misunderstandings.
- Critique the code or the idea, never the person.
- Be patient with people who are new, and with a maintainer who answers in the evening.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including someone's financial data from a
  bug report — without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues or discussions.

## Reporting

Write to **adrien.robino@outlook.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept
confidential. Anyone asked to stop is expected to stop; the maintainer may edit or remove
comments and block people who do not.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
