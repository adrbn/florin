# Florin trademark policy

Florin's **code** is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): use it, study it, change
it, share it, sell it. Florin's **name and logo** are not part of that grant. They identify
the app its author publishes, so that people who install "Florin" know whose work — and
whose privacy promise — they are getting.

This is the usual arrangement for open-source projects with a public face — Firefox and
Signal work this way — and the AGPL provides for it in section 7: see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## What is covered

- The name **Florin**, as the name of a personal-finance app or service.
- The **logo**: the gold coin with the fleur-de-lis, in every size and format — the app
  icons, `CoinFace`, and the icons under `apps/desktop` and `apps/web`.
- The **App Store and marketing images**: `apps/ios/AppStore/` and `.github/assets/`.

## What you can do without asking

- Download, use and share the **official, unmodified** builds from this repository, the
  App Store or TestFlight, under their name.
- **Fork, modify and redistribute the code** — under a different name and icon.
- **Refer to Florin truthfully**: "based on Florin", "a fork of Florin", "imports Florin
  backups", "compatible with a Florin server".
- Write about Florin, review it, and show its screenshots when you do.

## What needs a different name and icon

- Any **modified version**, free or paid, on any store or website.
- Any **hosted service** built on Florin's code.
- Anything that could make people think your version comes from, or is endorsed by,
  Florin's author.

Pick a name that is not confusingly close to "Florin", and an icon that is not a gold
coin with a fleur-de-lis. That is all it takes — the AGPL still gives you the code.

## Questions

Open an [issue](https://github.com/adrbn/florin/issues) if you are unsure whether a use is
fine. The answer is usually yes.
